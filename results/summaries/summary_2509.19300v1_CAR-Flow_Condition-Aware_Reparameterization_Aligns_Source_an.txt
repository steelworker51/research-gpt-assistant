### **1. Research Question/Hypothesis**
**Core Problem**:
Conditional generative models (e.g., diffusion/flow-matching) typically use a **condition-agnostic source distribution** (e.g., standard Gaussian noise), forcing the model to simultaneously:
1. **Transport probability mass** from the source to the target distribution.
2. **Inject conditional semantics** (e.g., class labels, text prompts) into the generated samples.

This dual burden **slows convergence, impairs sample quality, and increases training complexity**.

**Hypothesis**:
By introducing **condition-aware reparameterization (CAR-Flow)**—a lightweight, learned shift to the source, target, or both distributions—the model can:
- Shorten the "probability path" the velocity network must learn.
- Improve training efficiency and sample quality with minimal computational overhead.
