The main problem across these papers is improving the quality of 3D reconstruction from images or videos, particularly addressing limitations in memory and computational efficiency when scaling to high-resolution inputs. The proposed methods include operating in compressed video latent space to avoid pixel-space scaling [S3] and leveraging advanced reconstruction techniques. The papers evaluate performance using metrics like PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS, with results showing significant improvements over existing methods [S1][S4].
