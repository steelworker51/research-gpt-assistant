The main problem across these papers is improving the quality and efficiency of single-image or multi-view 3D reconstruction, particularly in handling high-resolution inputs and dynamic scenes. The proposed methods include leveraging compressed video latent spaces to avoid memory bottlenecks in attention mechanisms [S3], and utilizing advanced generative models to enhance reconstruction quality [S1]. Evaluations on benchmarks like RealEstate10K and Lyradataset show significant improvements in metrics like PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS [S1][S4]. The work also revisits depth representations for feed-forward 3D Gaussian splatting to optimize performance [S2].
