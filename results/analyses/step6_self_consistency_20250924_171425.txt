The main problem across these papers is improving the quality and efficiency of single-image or multi-view 3D reconstruction, particularly for dynamic scenes. The proposed method leverages a camera-controlled video diffusion model to operate in a compressed video latent space, avoiding the memory and compute bottlenecks of pixel-space attention mechanisms [S3]. It outperforms prior works on benchmarks like RealEstate10K and DL3DV, using metrics such as PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS [S1]. The approach also addresses dynamic 3D evaluation, significantly surpassing competitors like BTimer (GEN3C) on both static and dynamic datasets [S4]. The key innovation lies in scaling beyond GPU memory limits by working in latent space while maintaining high reconstruction quality.
