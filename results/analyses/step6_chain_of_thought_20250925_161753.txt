The main problem across these papers is the computational and memory limitations in generating high-quality 3D reconstructions from single images or multi-view videos, particularly due to the scalability issues of attention mechanisms in pixel space [S3]. The proposed method addresses this by operating in a compressed video latent space, leveraging a camera-controlled video diffusion model to synthesize a large number of high-resolution views without exceeding GPU memory limits [S3]. This approach significantly improves reconstruction quality, as demonstrated by superior performance on benchmarks like RealEstate10K and Tanks and Temples, with higher PSNR, SSIM, and lower LPIPS scores compared to prior works [S1, S4]. The method also outperforms existing dynamic 3D evaluation baselines like BTimer (GEN3C) on both static and dynamic datasets [S4].
